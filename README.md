# Tropical Masonry Repair Field Guide
## English / Filipino (Tagalog)

A practical field reference for assessing and repairing deteriorated 4-inch hollow concrete block walls in tropical climates. This project is intended to help local masons and site supervisors follow consistent repair practices while accounting for heavy rain, heat, humidity, and moisture exposure.

### Important safety notice
This is preliminary field guidance, not an engineer-approved structural repair design or a substitute for local building codes. If a wall is leaning, bulging, moving, severely cracked, load-bearing, or otherwise potentially unstable, stop work and obtain assessment from a qualified local professional before covering it with mortar or plaster.

### Quick thickness reference
- Masonry bed/head joints for rebuilding: about 10 mm, subject to the applicable specification.
- Leveling/render coats: commonly 10–15 mm per face on reasonably sound, prepared masonry, only where the selected product permits.
- Exterior render systems may specify different total thicknesses. Deep low spots should not automatically be filled in one thick pass.
- With a nominal 100 mm block and 10–15 mm render on each side, approximate finished thickness is 120–130 mm before finish coatings.

Always follow the selected product's instructions for substrate preparation, maximum coat thickness, curing, and finish compatibility.
